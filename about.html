About Sibusiso Electrical & Plumbing Services

One toolbox, then a team - built on referrals and reliable work.

Our Story

Sibusiso Electrical & Plumbing Services was started by Sibusiso Nkosi, a qualified electrician who began taking on plumbing work alongside electrical jobs after noticing how often customers needed both. What began as one man with a toolbox and a bakkie has grown into a small, trusted team serving homes and small businesses across Johannesburg.

To this day, almost all of our work comes from word-of-mouth referrals and repeat customers - this website is our first step toward a proper online presence, so new customers can find us just as easily as our regulars already do.

Our Mission

To deliver dependable, fairly-priced electrical and plumbing work with the same care and attention we'd want in our own homes.

Our Vision

To become Johannesburg's most recommended local electrical and plumbing service, one honest job and referral at a time.
